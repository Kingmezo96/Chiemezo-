EduBridge Initiative

An education support initiative connecting young people to learning tools, mentorship, and opportunity.

Detail

EduBridge is built to make educational access more practical, supported, and connected to real opportunity.

  • Client:EduBridge
  • Industry:Education & Youth Development
  • Category:Project Management
  • Duration:Program Cycle
